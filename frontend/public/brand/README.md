# Official FixIt Brand & Team Assets

### 1. Official FixIt Logo
Place your official logo image file here:
`/public/brand/fixit-logo.png`

The website's `FixItLogo` component (`src/components/brand/FixItLogo.tsx`) automatically detects and serves `/brand/fixit-logo.png`.
If this file is not found, it seamlessly renders the official stencil `#FIXIT` vector representation with the flame over the 'I', the pickaxe header, and the fountain pen nib at the base of the 'T'.

### 2. Official Team Portrait
Place the group portrait of the FixIt team here:
`/public/brand/team-portrait.png`
*(or at `/public/Three-Man #FIXIT Team Portrait.png`)*

The About Us page (`src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`) loads this image in an editorial-style archival frame beside "Our Story / How It Started".

### Supported Formats & Sizes
- **Logo:** `fixit-logo.png` (800px × 350px transparent or clean white background).
- **Team Portrait:** `team-portrait.png` or `Three-Man #FIXIT Team Portrait.png` (Square 1:1 original aspect ratio preserved).
